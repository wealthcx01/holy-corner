### HC-098 — An odd heading level and a status nobody recognises

**Status:** Frobnicated · **Phase:** ? · **Depends on:** —

An odd heading level (H3, not H1) and a status word that is not in the vocabulary. Both are the
author's mistake, not the parser's. The parser must read what it can, say what it could not, and
never throw: a ticket shown flagged is better than a ticket silently dropped off the board.
