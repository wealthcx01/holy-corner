## What this implies for the data model

- A **contract** has a counterparty entity, a type (MSA, Engagement Schedule, Collaboration & Incubation Agreement, Term Sheet, Service Schedule, Consultant Agreement, Founders Service Agreement), a parent contract (a schedule belongs to its MSA), dated and effective dates that can differ, a governing law, a status, and one or more PDF files.
- **Commercial terms are structured, not prose**: commission rate rows (engagement type, year-1 rate, year-2 rate, window months), a commitment payment (amount, currency, creditable yes/no), renewal rules, run-off, and for Foundry the milestone, equity split, vesting and long-stop.
- **Revenue events** come from terms: a commitment payment due on signature; commission accruing when the partner reports cash received from a Notified Prospect; a Brandfetch drawdown that reduces the receivable until USD 5,000 is consumed.
- An **invoice** is issued by a Bruntsfield entity to a counterparty entity against a contract term, has a number from a real sequence, and has a status that only Wise (or a human) can move to paid.
- A **Foundry venture** is a contract with no cash, so its "revenue" lines are milestone progress and Bruntsfield's funded spend — which Fountainbridge already tracks per department budget.
